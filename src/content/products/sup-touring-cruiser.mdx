## Built to Go the Distance

The Touring Cruiser SUP is crafted for paddlers who demand exceptional stability, straight-line tracking, and efficiency over long distances. Designed to deliver a smooth and steady ride, it minimizes flex and maximizes control, making it the ultimate companion for flat-water exploration and extended coastal cruising.

### Rigidity: The Rail Batten System

- **Innovative Rail Battens**: Advanced battens integrated directly into the side rails significantly reduce board flex
- **Stable, Efficient Glide**: A rigid platform that prevents sagging and maintains a steady pace stroke after stroke
- **Hard-Board Feel**: Low-flex construction delivers a smooth, confidence-inspiring ride that tracks arrow-straight

### Customizable Performance

- **Low-Profile US Fin Box**: A versatile, low-profile system that comes standard with an all-round paddling fin
- **9-Inch Racing/Touring Fin**: A premium fin that provides outstanding directional stability and tracking, keeping you on course with less effort
- **Aftermarket Ready**: Swap in your favorite flat-water or racing fins to tune the board for specific water conditions

### Product Features

- **Dual Cylinder Hand Pump**: A high-efficiency dual-cylinder system paired with an ergonomic handle rapidly moves high volume early and reaches higher pressures with less effort
- **12VDC Electric Pump Compatible**: Reach optimal pressure quickly with the optional afarer 12VDC electric pump
- **Streamlined Cargo Options**: Mount touring bags and hydration with bungee and D-ring tie-down points
- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Rigid, reinforced drop-stitch construction rated to 15 PSI
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, US box fin, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Long-Distance Touring & Cruising**: Cover miles smoothly with rigid rails that prevent sagging and keep an efficient glide
- **Flat-Water Exploration**: Serene lakes, calm bays, and slow-moving rivers where straight tracking and premium stability are paramount
- **Custom Performance Paddling**: Experienced paddlers can tune performance for specific conditions or racing with the adaptable US fin box system
- **Coastal Cruising Days**: Extended sessions along the shore with predictable tracking and minimal yaw

### Who It Is For

- Touring paddlers covering long distances
- Flat-water explorers and endurance cruisers
- Paddlers who want tunable performance via US fin compatibility
- OEM/ODM buyers sourcing a touring inflatable SUP board
