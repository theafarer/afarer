## Designed for Absolute Versatility

The Horizon Lines Series is the all-around inflatable SUP board built for ultimate versatility, effortless portability, and exceptional performance. Featuring a lightweight fusion laminate and an ultra-compact folding design, this board travels seamlessly without sacrificing durability or confidence on the water.

### Design Philosophy

Horizon Lines pairs a clean, modern deck aesthetic with a hull that delivers the perfect balance of comfort, adaptability, and high-performance engineering. The signature contoured kick pad provides precise tactile feedback for the rear foot, giving paddlers superior grip and confident board control in every condition.

### Construction

- **Lightweight Fusion Laminate**: Specialized reinforcement layers in key areas strike an optimized balance of featherlight handling and long-lasting durability
- **Reptile-Skin Traction Pad**: Unparalleled comfort with a signature contoured WF kick pad and arch support for confident foot placement
- **Triple Click Fin Setup**: Three fully removable click fins with optimized toe-in angle let you customize your setup for any water condition

### Performance

- All-around hull geometry delivers responsive handling from serene flatwater to choppy coastal conditions
- Optimized toe-in fin angle provides balanced tracking and agile maneuverability
- Rigid drop-stitch construction rated to 15 PSI for a hard-shell feel

### Product Features

- **Dual Inflation Options**: Double-action high-pressure/high-volume pump inflates on both strokes, or use a 12VDC 20PSI electric pump to reach 15 PSI in about 9 minutes
- **Premium Utility D-Rings**: High-grade stainless steel D-rings at nose, tail, and forward deck for towing, leash attachment, and cargo storage
- **Travel-Ready Compactness**: Folds compactly for effortless transportation and storage on the move
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Calm Lakes**: Stable flatwater platform for cruising and fitness paddling
- **Moving Rivers**: Fully capable of handling moving water and gentle currents with the optional triple-fin adjustment
- **Coastal Waters**: Adaptable to choppy conditions by tuning the removable fin configuration
- **Advanced Maneuver Zones**: Responsive rear-foot tactile feedback supports bottom turns, tail drops, and submerged tail pivot turns
- **Whitewater & River Touring**: Handles moving river water when paired with mandatory safety gear such as a quick-release leash, helmet, and buoyant life jacket

### Who It Is For

- Paddlers seeking one board that handles any adventure
- Flatwater cruisers and advanced maneuver enthusiasts
- Travelers who need a compact, packable SUP
- Retailers and wholesalers looking for a versatile all-around inflatable paddle board line
