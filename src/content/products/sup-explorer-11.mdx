## Your first SUP, perfected

The SUP Explorer 11' is our most popular all-around inflatable SUP board - wide enough for absolute beginners, agile enough for intermediate paddlers, and portable enough for any adventure. Whether you are exploring calm lakes, meandering slow rivers, or paddling sheltered coastal waters, this board delivers the confidence and performance you need.

### Construction and Durability

Built with military-grade drop-stitch PVC core construction rated to 15 PSI, the Explorer delivers a rigid platform that rivals hard-shell paddle boards. The 32-inch wide deck provides superior lateral stability, making it one of the most stable inflatable SUPs in its class. Reinforced rail bands protect against impact from docks, rocks, and shorelines.

### Complete Package

Every SUP Explorer 11' ships as a complete paddle board package:

- SUP board with removable center fin
- Adjustable aluminum 3-piece paddle
- Dual-action hand pump with integrated pressure gauge
- Coiled safety leash with comfortable ankle cuff
- Premium travel backpack with padded shoulder straps and fin pocket
- Repair kit with PVC patches and valve tool

### Product Features

- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Military-grade woven polyester drop-stitch construction rated to 15 PSI for a rigid, hard-shell feel
- **32-Inch Wide Deck**: Optimized width for superior lateral stability, confidence for beginners, and comfort for yoga
- **Reinforced Rail Bands**: Double-layer sidewall protection against dock scrapes, shoreline rocks, and impact
- **2+1 Fin Configuration**: Dual molded side fins for straight tracking plus detachable center fin for maneuverability
- **Six PVC D-Rings**: Nose bungee rigging (4 D-rings) for securing dry bags and gear, plus rear tie-downs for accessories
- **Three Comfort Handles**: Center neoprene-wrapped carry handle plus front and rear haul handles
- **Bravo Inflation Valve**: High-flow valve for quick inflation and deflation, compatible with standard SUP pumps
- **Complete Package**: Includes SUP board, 3-piece adjustable aluminum paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Lakes and Reservoirs**: Glassy flatwater conditions ideal for fitness paddling, yoga, and family outings
- **Slow Rivers**: Gentle current navigation with responsive tracking
- **Sheltered Coastal Waters**: Protected bays and inlets with mild chop conditions
- **Travel and Camping**: Deflates and packs into a compact backpack for car trunks, RVs, or checked luggage

### Who It Is For

- First-time paddlers and recreational users looking for a reliable inflatable SUP
- Fitness enthusiasts practicing yoga and paddle fitness on flatwater
- Families wanting a shared board stable enough for riders of all skill levels
- Adventure travelers who need a portable SUP board for camping and road trips
