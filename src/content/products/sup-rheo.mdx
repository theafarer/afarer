## The Bone of Speed, The Soul of Flow

The RHEO Series is the ultimate elite-tier race inflatable SUP board, designed for competitive racers, speed enthusiasts, and experienced paddlers who refuse to compromise. Engineered to deliver a true hardboard-like feel, it bridges inflatable convenience with podium-topping performance.

### Design Philosophy

The RHEO design philosophy is summed up in a single line: the bone of speed, the soul of flow. It merges hardcore racing performance with a vibrant, modern color aesthetic, breaking away from the dull industrial feel of traditional racing paddleboards. The refined "First Light" color matrix, grounded in a clean cream-white base with deep plum, coral orange, and cherry blossom pink accents, radiates a warm and confident competitive spirit.

### Hull Design

- **Carbon Hybrid Inflatable Construction**: A cutting-edge carbon hybrid matrix delivers unprecedented rigidity, eliminating flex entirely for a rock-solid, ultra-stiff platform
- **Wave-Piercing Displacement Nose**: The razor-sharp nose slices through water with minimal drag, providing exceptional penetration in head chop and crosswinds
- **Golden Racing Proportion**: The extreme 14-foot length delivers unparalleled straight-line tracking and maximum glide distance with every stroke
- **Aggressive Glide Rocker**: A finely tuned, performance-driven rocker slices through water with minimal resistance, maximizing forward momentum

### Performance

- The professional 26-inch narrow width reduces wetted surface friction for higher cadence and more efficient power transfer
- 2+1 fin setup maintains excellent straight-line tracking at high speeds while offering lateral stability during fast, sweeping turns
- Streamlined silhouette allows you to navigate tighter pockets, hold tight lines, and sustain peak velocity
- Refined all-around racing geometry keeps the board highly agile for sharp, precise buoy turns

### Product Features

- **Split EVA Deck Pad**: Dark, slip-resistant traction is highly dirt-resistant with the grooving aligned parallel to water flow
- **Lightweight Front Bungee System**: Streamlined cargo system for athletes carrying hydration and essentials during long-distance training
- **Dynamic Streamline Gradients**: Deck stripes simulate the water parting and converging along the rails at speed
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Competitive SUP Racing & Sprints**: Perfect for official race circuits, technical sprint races, and time trials where every second counts
- **High-Speed Fitness Training**: Ideal for high-intensity cardio sessions with the speed and tracking needed for long, aggressive training
- **Challenging & Tight Waterways**: Excels in narrow channels and technical courses requiring rapid acceleration and sharp maneuverability

### Who It Is For

- Competitive racers chasing personal bests
- Speed enthusiasts and advanced paddlers
- Athletes seeking hardboard-like rigidity with inflatable portability
