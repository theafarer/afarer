## Still Waters Run Deep, and All Things Co-Exist in Harmony

The Flowing Lotus Series seamlessly blends traditional Eastern brushwork with modern outdoor water sports, creating an inflatable SUP board that inspires tranquility on every outing.

### Design Philosophy

The front features a vibrant gradient mimicking early morning sunlight dancing on water, with intertwining shades of pink, orange, green, and blue capturing the dynamic rhythm of flowing water. Clean generous teal in the middle and rear sections creates perfect visual balance between movement and stillness.

### Craftsmanship

- **Laser-Engraved Traction Pad**: Dragonfly and lotus patterns etched into dual-layer composite EVA — never wears out or fades
- **Scaled Production Details**: Core elements enlarged and streamlined for flawless UV digital printing at scale
- **High-Elastic Flexible Ink**: Gradient water-flow patterns resist cracking at 15 PSI and repeated folding
- **Dynamic Fin System**: Yellow and blue color-blocked side fins for excellent tracking

### Performance

- All-around hull with widened midsection and upturned nose rocker
- 2+1 fin configuration for balanced tracking
- Ample tolerance zones around D-rings and handle for flawless assembly

### Product Features

- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Military-grade construction rated to 15 PSI for rigid performance
- **32-Inch Wide Deck**: All-around hull with upturned nose rocker for chop-slicing
- **2+1 Fin Configuration**: Color-blocked side fins plus detachable center fin for excellent tracking
- **Bravo Inflation Valve**: High-flow valve for rapid inflation with standard SUP pumps
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Lakes and Ponds**: Mirror-flat water for yoga, meditation, and mindful paddling
- **Slow Rivers**: Gentle current perfect for contemplative journeys
- **Sheltered Waters**: Protected coves and calm inlets
- **Wellness Retreats**: Designed for paddle yoga, breathwork, and water-based mindfulness practices

### Who It Is For

- Paddlers who appreciate Eastern aesthetics and traditional art
- Yoga and meditation practitioners seeking a stable SUP platform
- Those who paddle for relaxation and mental wellness
- Art lovers who want their equipment to reflect their aesthetic values
