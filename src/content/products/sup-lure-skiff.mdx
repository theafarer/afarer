## Beyond the Ordinary SUP

The Utility & Lure Skiff SUP is not a typical paddle board. Its most prominent feature is a massive square cutout in the center of the deck, a design that is extremely rare in conventional SUPs. It transforms the board into a dedicated platform for lure fishing, underwater observation, and heavy-duty utility work.

### Central Cutout Compartment

- **Fish-Viewing / Underwater Window**: Pairs with a transparent, high-definition tempered glass or acrylic window so lure anglers can watch water flow, underwater structure, and fish movement beneath them
- **Livewell & Gear Retrofit**: Use the cutout to install a live bait tank, store landing nets, or retrieve lines while seated without rubbing the line against the board
- **Seated Angling Convenience**: Easier line handling and a comfortable, stable seating position for long fight sessions

### Ultra-Wide Pontoon Stability

- **47-Inch Profile**: Standard all-around boards measure 80-84 cm in width; this board widens to 120 cm for unmatched lateral stability
- **Stubby, Wide Outline**: A robust, pontoon-like silhouette that is virtually impossible to flip, even when casting, setting the hook, or fighting large fish
- **Swallowtail Stern with Dual-Thruster Setup**: Excellent maneuverability and responsiveness despite the extreme width, with pre-configured mounting space for power propulsion systems

### Massive Load Capacity

The heavy-duty, reinforced drop-stitch construction is rated for an enormous 880 lb load. It handles the angler's weight plus coolers, rod holders, lithium batteries, and even an external electric trolling motor with buoyancy to spare.

### Product Features

- **Abundant Handles & D-Rings**: Multiple heavy-duty grab handles on the sides make carrying easy over rough terrain and offer secure handholds in the water
- **Multi-Fin System**: A tri-fin configuration significantly improves straight-line tracking and wind resistance on a wide hull
- **Front Bungee System & Action Mounts**: Drop-stitch D-rings and a bungee cargo net at the nose, plus pre-equipped black EVA action mounts for rod holders and fish finder mounts
- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Rigid, reinforced construction rated to 15 PSI

### Application Environments

- **Lure Fishing & Fly Angling**: Cast, set, fight, and land fish from a rock-solid standing or seated platform
- **Underwater Reconnaissance**: Glass the shallows through the viewing window for structure and bait movement
- **Heavy-Duty Hauling**: Tow or carry coolers, tackle, and powered gear for long excursions
- **Stable Utility Platform**: Use it as a swim deck, camera platform, or camp staging raft

### Who It Is For

- Lure and fly anglers who want kayak-like utility with SUP portability
- Spearfishers and shallow-water observers
- Anglers running trolling motors from an inflatable platform
- OEM/ODM buyers sourcing a multi-purpose lure skiff SUP
