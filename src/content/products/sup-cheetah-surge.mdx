## Conquering the Wild with Fluid Strength

The Cheetah Surge Edition is a celebration of raw, wild power meeting artistic elegance. This design merges the predatory agility of a cheetah with the primal energy of the ocean, creating an inflatable SUP board that performs as boldly as it looks.

### Design Philosophy

The top deck is an untamed wilderness, featuring an intricate geometrically textured cheetah illustration bordered by bold ocean flora and a contrast-colored solid white silhouette. The bottom is a minimalist study in movement with a clean pink field and the wave-infused afarer branding.

### Craftsmanship

- **Multi-Color EVA Block Piecing**: The traction pad artwork uses die-cut precision rather than printed ink —zero fade, zero peeling, even under heavy use
- **High-Precision UV Digital Printing**: Nose artwork delivers vibrant color saturation and silky-smooth fine lines
- **Anti-Delamination Design**: D-ring attachment areas are masked for raw PVC-to-PVC bonding

### Performance

- All-around hull with widened 32-inch midsection for superior lateral stability
- Gently upturned nose rocker slices through chops and surface ripples
- 2+1 fin configuration (dual molded side fins + detachable center fin) for balanced tracking and maneuverability

### Product Features

- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Military-grade construction rated to 15 PSI for rigid, hard-shell performance
- **32-Inch Wide Deck**: All-around hull optimized for stability, maneuverability, and paddler confidence
- **2+1 Fin Configuration**: Dual molded side fins plus detachable center fin for balanced tracking
- **Bravo Inflation Valve**: High-flow valve for rapid inflation and deflation with standard SUP pumps
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Lakes and Reservoirs**: Stable platform for flatwater cruising and fitness paddling
- **Slow Rivers**: Responsive handling in gentle currents
- **Sheltered Coastal Waters**: Confident performance in mild chop and protected bays
- **Photo and Video Destinations**: Striking design makes every outing a visual statement

### Who It Is For

- Paddlers who want a board that stands out on the water
- Flatwater cruisers and recreational paddlers
- Fitness enthusiasts and yoga practitioners
- Social-media conscious adventurers and content creators
