## Small Board, Endless Possibilities

The Mini SUP Series breaks the mold of traditional paddle boards by delivering an ultra-versatile, multi-purpose hybrid design. It transitions seamlessly between a compact paddle board, an inflatable surfboard, and a high-performance bodyboard, engineered for maximum utility on any water toy adventure.

### Design Philosophy

Don't let its compact size fool you. While it serves as the perfect introductory board for younger riders, its robust buoyancy and playful shape make it an equally exciting wave-riding tool for adults and a fun floating deck for four-legged companions.

### Multi-Purpose Hybrid Geometry

- **Short, Wide, Highly Stable Profile**: Adapts fluidly to stand-up paddling, prone bodyboarding, and wave riding
- **Optimized Volume-to-Length Ratio**: Maximum buoyancy and stability within a compact footprint that supports everyone from children to fully-grown adults
- **Ultra-Portable Footprint**: Deflates and rolls into an incredibly small package, making it the most travel-friendly and quick-to-inflate board in the lineup

### Product Features

- **Dual-Age Versatility**: A dedicated kids SUP and surfboard that is easy to maneuver, yet rigid enough for adult bodyboarding or rescue duty
- **Durable, Pet-Friendly Deck**: Heavy-duty, puncture-resistant materials withstand the claws and paws of dogs
- **Dynamic Wave Adaptation**: A responsive rocker line and rail profile catch small ocean waves or river breaks, standing up or prone
- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Rigid drop-stitch construction rated to 15 PSI with a lightweight, easy-to-carry weight
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Kids' SUP & Surf Progression**: A manageable starter board for kids and teens learning to balance, paddle, and catch their first waves
- **Surf Zones & Shorebreaks**: A high-buoyancy platform for adults charging shorebreaks and small surf
- **Lakes, Pools & Calm Bays**: A safe, balanced floating pad for pet owners and family play days

### Who It Is For

- Parents and families introducing kids to paddle sports
- Adults wanting a portable surf or bodyboard hybrid
- Pet owners who bring their dogs on the water
- OEM/ODM buyers sourcing a mini inflatable SUP paddle board
