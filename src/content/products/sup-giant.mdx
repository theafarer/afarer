## One Board, A Whole Crew

The Multi-Person Giant SUP is a large-format team board built to bring groups together on the water. With extreme volume, an extra-wide deck, and a reinforced hull, it turns family gatherings, team building, and rental outings into unforgettable group adventures.

### Construction & Dimensions

- **8-Inch Hull Thickness**: Required for extreme volume, keeping the board rigid and stable under heavy, concentrated loads
- **59-Inch Wide Deck**: Maximum lateral stability when multiple paddlers shift their weight at the same time
- **Swallowtail or Squared-Off Tail**: Provides a firm, stable footprint and predictable ride as the crew moves around

### Inflation & Rigidity

- **Dual-Valve System**: Two people can inflate the giant board at the same time thanks to the massive internal air volume
- **Operating Pressure 11-15 PSI**: Rigid drop-stitch construction rated up to 15 PSI for hard-shell feel
- **Heavy-Duty Drop-Stitch PVC**: Reinforced materials stand up to concentrated loads and rough handling

### Product Features

- **Multi-Handle Configuration**: 8 to 12 heavy-duty neoprene grab handles down both rails plus nose and tail handles for team carrying, launching, and re-boarding
- **Multi-Fin System**: 4+1 or tri-fin setup keeps the wide board tracking straight and reduces lateral drift from current and wind
- **Double Cargo Bungee Systems**: 4-point or 6-point bungee nets at nose and tail secure dry bags, coolers, and safety gear
- **Multiple D-Rings & Leash Attachments**: Heavy-duty stainless steel D-rings at the tail corners let multiple riders leash up without tangling, plus a nose ring for towing or anchoring

### Application Environments

- **Corporate Team Building**: A floating platform for bonding events and group challenges
- **Commercial Rentals & Club Excursions**: A reliable crowd-pleaser for water sports rental fleets and SUP clubs
- **Large Family Gatherings**: Multi-kid lake parties and group holidays on one stable deck
- **Experimental Crew Sessions**: Yoga classes, fishing trips, and paddle tours with a whole team aboard

### Who It Is For

- Rental operators and watersports clubs
- Event organizers running team-building programs
- Large families and group adventurers
- OEM/ODM buyers sourcing a giant multi-person inflatable SUP board
